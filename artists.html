Vincent van Gogh

A Dutch Post-Impressionist painter who became one of the most famous and influential figures in Western art history. He created about 2,100 artworks, including famous masterpieces like "The Starry Night" and "Sunflowers."

Claude Monet

A founder and key figure of French Impressionist painting. Monet specialized in plein-air landscape painting, capturing the changing qualities of light and color, and became famous for his series of Water Lilies paintings.

rembrandt van rign

A 17th-century Dutch painter, draughtsman, and printmaker. Widely considered one of the greatest visual artists in history and the Dutch Golden Age, he is famous for his dramatic use of light and shadow in works like "The Night Watch."
